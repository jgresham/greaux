## Revenue Category Drivers

1. **Transaction-Based Revenues** — Payment for order flow (PFOF) on equities and options; crypto
   spread revenue on crypto trades. Subject to regulatory risk (SEC PFOF rules). Crypto component
   is highly cyclical. Options is the most stable and consistently growing sub-category.
2. **Net Interest Revenues** — Earned on: (a) margin loan balances; (b) stock lending program
   (customer shares lent to short sellers); (c) uninvested cash sweep into money market funds
   (Robinhood earns a spread); (d) interest on customer cash. Sensitive to Fed funds rate and AUC.
3. **Other Revenues** — Robinhood Gold subscription ($5/month or $50/year; perks include 5% APY
   on uninvested cash, 3% IRA match, premium research, higher instant deposit limits);
   proxy/transfer agent fees; cash card interchange; emerging services.

## Caveats

- Transaction revenue is highly volatile due to crypto component. CY2024 crypto revenue was $672M
  vs. $126M in CY2023 — a 5x swing in a single year.
- GAAP net income in CY2024 (47.8% margin) is likely unsustainable; normalized margin will be lower
  as headcount/R&D investments resume and crypto tailwinds normalize.
- Robinhood does not report segment operating income. All profitability is company-wide GAAP.
- PFOF regulatory risk: SEC and FINRA have raised concerns about payment for order flow.
  A full PFOF ban (as in UK/Canada) would significantly impact options and equities transaction revenue.
