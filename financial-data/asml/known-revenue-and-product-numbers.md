# ASML Known Revenue And Product Numbers

## Summary

ASML Holding N.V. (ASML) reports revenue in three categories: EUV Systems, DUV & Other Systems, and
Installed Base Management (field services + upgrade options). Data covers CY2022–CY2024.
All figures from ASML Q4 earnings press releases. Revenue reported in EUR billions.

## Product Or Segment Drivers

1. **EUV Systems** — Extreme Ultraviolet lithography tools (NXE:3400/3600/3800 and High-NA EXE:5000).
   Required for all leading-edge nodes (7nm and below). ASML is the sole global supplier.
   High-NA EUV (EXE:5000) began first customer shipments in CY2024; Intel was first recipient.
2. **DUV & Other Systems** — Deep Ultraviolet lithography (immersion, dry) plus metrology and inspection tools.
   Used for mature and mid-range nodes. China demand was a significant driver through 2023 before
   Dutch/US export restrictions tightened. Includes TWINSCAN NXT (immersion) and XT (dry) systems.
3. **Installed Base Management** — Field services, maintenance contracts, and upgrade options for
   the global ASML tool fleet. Revenue grows predictably with the installed base. Each EUV system
   generates substantial annual service revenue over its ~20-year operational lifespan.

## Caveats

- ASML reports all financials in EUR. USD-equivalent figures vary with EUR/USD exchange rate.
- Revenue-recognized units (from backlog) differ from shipment counts; ASML often ships in late quarters
  with revenue-recognition in the following quarter.
- DUV revenue in CY2023 was inflated by China pull-forward orders ahead of export controls.
- "DUV & Other Systems" includes a small amount of High-NA EUV (EXE:5000) systems beginning in CY2024.
