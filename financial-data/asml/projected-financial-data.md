# ASML Projected Financial Data

## Default Projection Inputs

| Input | Default Value | Source / Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EUV CAGR | 15% | High-NA EUV (EXE:5000) ramp at Intel 18A/14A, TSMC 2nm/1.4nm, and Samsung drives units from 44 (2024) toward ~80+ by 2029. Low-NA capacity also expanding. Source: ASML 2025 investor day targets, analyst consensus. |
| DUV CAGR | 5% | China export controls cap upside; mature node demand from trailing-edge expansion provides a floor. 5% reflects slow growth with continued restrictions. Source: analyst consensus. |
| Services CAGR | 10% | Installed base management grows predictably as the global EUV+DUV fleet expands. Each EUV generates €40-50M/year in service revenue. Source: ASML investor presentations. |
| GAAP net margin | 27% | CY2024 was 26.8%. Modest expansion as EUV gross margins improve with High-NA scale; offset by R&D investment. Source: ASML long-range targets. |
| P/E multiple | 30x | ASML has traded 25–40x. 30x is near the low end due to EUV cycle uncertainty and geopolitical exposure (China restrictions). Source: Bloomberg consensus. |
| Diluted shares | 0.394B | CY2024 diluted shares ~393.6M. ASML runs a systematic buyback programme. Source: ASML Annual Report CY2024. |
