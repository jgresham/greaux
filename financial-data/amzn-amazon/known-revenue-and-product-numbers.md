# Amazon Known Revenue And Product Numbers

## Summary

Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) reports three geographic/business reportable segments (North America, International, AWS)
but also publicly discloses revenue in six product/service categories. Data covers CY2022–CY2024.
All figures from Amazon Form 10-K filings. Dashboard uses the six product-line categories plus an aggregated "Stores" line.

## Product Or Segment Drivers

1. **AWS** — cloud computing platform; largest source of operating profit at $57.7B in CY2024 despite being ~17% of net sales.
2. **Advertising Services** — sponsored products, sponsored brands, display/DSP, Prime Video ads (launched Jan 2024). Fastest-growing high-margin segment.
3. **Third-Party Seller Services** — marketplace commissions (referral fees), Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA), and multi-channel fulfillment. Largest revenue category at $159B.
4. **Subscription Services** — Amazon Prime (fast shipping, Prime Video, music), Kindle Unlimited, Audible, Amazon Kids+.
5. **Stores (Online + Physical + Other)** — direct product sales (amazon.com), Whole Foods, Amazon Fresh, Amazon Go, Amazon Pharmacy, Amazon Freight, and emerging services.

## Caveats

- Amazon does not disclose operating income by the product-line revenue categories; only by geographic/business segments (North America, International, AWS).
- The "Other" category in product-line reporting ($9.9B in CY2024) includes Amazon Freight, Pharmacy, Advertising technology licensing, and other. It has grown significantly.
- Amazon GAAP net income was deeply negative in CY2022 primarily due to a $12.7B unrealized Rivian investment loss.
