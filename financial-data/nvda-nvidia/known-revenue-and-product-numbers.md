## Key Financial Metrics (FY2025)
- Total Revenue: $130,497M
- Gross Profit: $98,314M (gross margin 75.3%)
- Net Income: $72,880M (net margin 55.8%)
- Diluted EPS: $2.94
- Diluted shares: ~24.8B
- Free Cash Flow: ~$60.8B

## Data Center Product Notes
- H100 SXM / PCIe (Hopper architecture) — primary revenue driver FY2024
- H200 (HBM3e upgrade) — ramped in H2 FY2025
- Blackwell B200 / GB200 NVL72 — began volume shipments FY2025 Q4
- NVLink switch domains for multi-GPU clusters (up to 576 GPUs per domain)
- DGX Cloud / AI Enterprise software attach

## Automotive
- DRIVE Thor SoC design wins announced at GTC 2025 (BYD, Toyota, Volvo, others)
- DRIVE Orin still in production ramp across existing design wins
- Nvidia guided automotive to $5B revenue by FY2026

## Gaming
- GeForce RTX 40 series (Ada Lovelace) — mainstream through FY2025
- GeForce RTX 50 series (Blackwell) — announced Jan 2025 CES, shipping 2025

## Professional Visualization
- RTX 6000 Ada workstation GPU
- Omniverse Enterprise platform
- Isaac Sim for robotics and digital twin workflows
