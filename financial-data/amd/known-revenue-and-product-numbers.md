# AMD Known Revenue and Product Numbers

## Summary

AMD reports four segments since the Xilinx acquisition closed February 14, 2022: Data Center, Client, Gaming, and Embedded. All figures are calendar-year (AMD fiscal year = calendar year).

## Known Official Historical Data (USD billions, from AMD 10-K filings)

| Year | Data Center | Client | Gaming | Embedded | Total  | Source |
|------|-------------|--------|--------|----------|--------|--------|
| 2022 | 6.04        | 6.20   | 6.81   | 4.56     | 23.60  | AMD FY2022 10-K. FY2022 includes ~10.5 months of Xilinx (acquisition closed Feb 14, 2022). |
| 2023 | 6.50        | 4.64   | 5.84   | 5.70     | 22.68  | AMD FY2023 10-K. First full year with Xilinx embedded segment. |
| 2024 | 12.66       | 7.07   | 2.80   | 3.24     | 25.77  | AMD FY2024 10-K. MI300X ramp drove Data Center step-change. |

Note: 2022 and 2023 segment figures are from the 10-K but should be verified directly against the filings — the embedded figures in particular may differ slightly due to rounding or restatements.

## Segment Definitions

- **Data Center**: EPYC server CPUs (Genoa/Bergamo/Siena/Turin), Instinct accelerators (MI300X/MI350/MI325X), DPUs, cloud-inference platforms, and AMD ROCm software.
- **Client**: Ryzen desktop and laptop CPUs (including Ryzen AI), Radeon discrete client GPUs, and AMD integrated graphics.
- **Gaming**: Semi-custom SoCs for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, and discrete Radeon gaming GPUs (RX 7000 series).
- **Embedded**: Versal adaptive SoCs, Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC, Artix/Kintex/Virtex FPGAs, and automotive-grade products (all Xilinx product lines). Used in industrial, communications, automotive, aerospace, and data-center FPGA workloads.

## Key Financial Metrics (FY2024, from 10-K)

- Total Revenue: $25,785M
- GAAP Net Income: $1,641M (GAAP net margin ~6.4%)
- Non-GAAP Net Income: ~$6,300M (non-GAAP net margin ~24.4%)
- Diluted EPS (GAAP): ~$1.01
- Diluted shares: ~1,617M (~1.62B)
- Stock-based compensation + intangible amortization (Xilinx): ~$4.3B in FY2024

## Operating Metrics

- EPYC server CPU: Estimated 20–24% x86 server CPU market share in H2 2024 (Mercury Research estimates; not officially reported by AMD).
- MI300X: AMD shipped >$5B of MI300 series in FY2024. Key customers include Microsoft Azure, Meta, Oracle Cloud.
- Gaming: Semi-custom revenue is contract-driven. Current PlayStation 5 / Xbox Series X/S cycle began in late 2020; next-gen consoles expected mid-to-late 2026.
- Embedded: Channel inventory digestion began H2 2022; AMD guided to "stabilization" in 2024 and growth recovery in 2025.

## Caveats

- GAAP net income is significantly depressed by ~$3–4B/year in Xilinx acquisition-related intangible amortization. This amortizes down through ~2026–2027. Non-GAAP margins are more representative of ongoing earning power.
- AMD does not report gross margin by segment publicly.
- Embedded 2022 figure ($4.56B) only covers ~10.5 months of Xilinx operation under AMD ownership.
