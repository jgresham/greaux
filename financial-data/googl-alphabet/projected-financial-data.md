# Alphabet Projected Financial Data

App source file: `src/pages/Google.jsx`

## Default Projection Inputs

| Input | Default Value | Source / Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search CAGR | 10% | Search grew ~12-15% in 2023-2024 but faces AI assistant headwinds and market maturity. 10% is a moderate base case. Source: Wall Street consensus, Bernstein GOOGL note 2024. |
| YouTube Ads CAGR | 12% | YouTube re-accelerating after flat 2022. Connected TV, Shorts monetization, and sports rights support ~12% annualized. Source: analyst consensus. |
| Google Cloud CAGR | 25% | GCP has been gaining cloud market share (from ~10% to ~13%). Smaller base than AWS/Azure supports faster growth. Gemini integration driving enterprise adoption. Source: IDC cloud market share, Gartner. |
| Subscriptions & Platforms CAGR | 12% | Google One 100M+ paid subscribers, YouTube Premium growth, Workspace expansion. Source: analyst estimates, reported Google One milestone. |
| Google Network CAGR | 3% | Structural headwinds from cookie deprecation and first-party signal shift. Very slow growth or flat. Source: eMarketer programmatic ad market forecasts. |
| GAAP net margin | 28% | CY2024 GAAP net margin was 28.6%. Cloud segment profitability improvement offset by AI investment. Source: Alphabet 10-K CY2024. |
| P/E multiple | 22x | Alphabet traded at 20–25x forward earnings in 2024. 22x is toward the low end of recent range. Source: Bloomberg consensus. |
| Diluted shares | 12.3B | CY2024 diluted share count ~12.3B. Alphabet authorized $70B+ buyback in 2024. Source: Alphabet 10-K CY2024. |
