# Alphabet Known Revenue And Product Numbers

## Summary

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) reports revenue in three reportable segments: Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other Bets.
For dashboard purposes, Google Services is further broken down into its publicly disclosed revenue line items.
Data covers CY2022–CY2024. All figures from Alphabet Form 10-K and Form 8-K earnings releases.

## Product Or Segment Drivers

1. **Google Search & other** — core paid search advertising (Google.com, Shopping, Maps, Google Images); largest revenue line at $198B.
2. **YouTube Ads** — video advertising on YouTube; includes brand/display + direct response + shorts ads.
3. **Google Cloud** — infrastructure (GCP IaaS/PaaS), data analytics (BigQuery), Vertex AI, Google Workspace B2B.
4. **Subscriptions, Platforms & Devices** — Google One (cloud storage subscriptions), YouTube Premium, Pixel hardware, Google Workspace consumer, app/in-app purchases.
5. **Google Network** — Google Network Members' properties: AdSense, AdMob, Google Ad Manager (third-party publisher monetization).

## Caveats

- Alphabet does not report Google Search or YouTube operating income separately.
- "Other Bets" (~$1.6B in CY2024) covers Waymo, Verily, DeepMind commercialization, etc. and is excluded from the dashboard model as immaterial.
- Google Cloud operating margin was negative until CY2023.
