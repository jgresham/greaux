# Microsoft Known Revenue And Product Numbers

## Summary

Microsoft reports three reportable segments: Intelligent Cloud, Productivity and Business Processes,
and More Personal Computing. Data covers Microsoft's fiscal years FY2022–FY2024
(Microsoft's fiscal year ends June 30). All figures from Form 10-K filings.

## Product Or Segment Drivers

1. **Intelligent Cloud** — Azure cloud platform (IaaS, PaaS, AI), Windows Server, SQL Server, GitHub. Azure is the primary growth engine.
2. **Productivity and Business Processes** — Microsoft 365 (formerly Office 365), LinkedIn, Dynamics 365 (ERP/CRM), Power Platform.
3. **More Personal Computing** — Windows OEM licensing, Surface devices, Xbox hardware and content, Bing and Microsoft advertising, Activision Blizzard games (post-Oct 2023 acquisition).

## Caveats

- Microsoft fiscal year ends June 30 (not December 31).
- Activision Blizzard acquisition (Oct 2023) added ~$2B/quarter to More Personal Computing via gaming revenue. FY2024 includes ~3 quarters of Activision.
- Azure growth is disclosed as a percentage, not an absolute dollar figure, in Microsoft's 10-K.
