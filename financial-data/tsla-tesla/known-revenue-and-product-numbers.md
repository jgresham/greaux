## Product And Capacity Notes

From the FY 2025 Update:

- Automotive installed annual capacity: California Model 3/Y greater than 550,000; California Model S/X 100,000; Shanghai Model 3/Y greater than 950,000; Berlin Model Y greater than 375,000; Texas Model Y greater than 250,000; Cybertruck greater than 125,000.
- Cybercab and Tesla Semi were listed as tooling; Roadster was listed as design development.
- Energy installed annual capacity: California Megapack 40 GWh; Nevada Powerwall greater than 6 GWh; Shanghai Megapack 40 GWh; Texas Megapack under construction.
- Robotics: California Optimus under construction.
- Tesla said Optimus Gen 3 is the first design meant for mass production, with start of production planned before the end of 2026 and eventual planned capacity of 1 million robots per year.
- In 2025, Tesla said its global Powerwall network supported more than 89,000 Virtual Power Plant events across over 1 million installed units.

## Data Hygiene Notes

- Treat annual revenue, production, delivery, storage deployment, and Supercharger figures as reported actuals.
- Treat capacity and product roadmap items as company-stated status, not realized production.
- Tesla may reclassify or restate prior-period balances. Prefer the latest annual update or SEC filing when a number differs across Tesla documents.
