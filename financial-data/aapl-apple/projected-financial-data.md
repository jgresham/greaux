# Apple Projected Financial Data

App source file: `src/pages/Apple.jsx`

## Default Projection Inputs

| Input | Default Value | Source / Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone CAGR (from FY2024) | 5% | Consensus ~4–6%; AI iPhone supercycle thesis supports mid-single-digit growth; India expansion adds ~1–2%. Source: Morgan Stanley, Wedbush analyst estimates. |
| Services CAGR (from FY2024) | 14% | Services has grown at 14–17% per year for several years. Deceleration expected as base matures; Apple Intelligence monetization upside. Source: Apple guidance, analyst consensus. |
| Mac CAGR (from FY2024) | 6% | Post-M3 cycle normalization; modest refresh cadence. Source: IDC PC market forecasts. |
| iPad CAGR (from FY2024) | 4% | Flat category; modest AI-use upside. Source: IDC tablet forecasts. |
| Wearables CAGR (from FY2024) | 5% | AirPods market leadership; Apple Watch growth moderating. Source: analyst consensus. |
| GAAP net margin | 25% | FY2024 was ~24.0%. Services mix shift should expand margins. Source: Apple 10-K, Wall Street consensus. |
| P/E multiple | 30x | Apple trades at 28–33x forward earnings. Default of 30x is near midpoint. Source: Bloomberg consensus as of 2024. |
| Diluted shares | 13.5B | Apple had ~15.1B diluted shares Q4 FY2024 but actively buying back. ~13.5B by FY2029 is achievable at recent buyback pace. Source: Apple 10-K, analyst models. |
