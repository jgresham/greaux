# Apple Known Revenue And Product Numbers

## Summary

Apple reports five revenue segments: iPhone, Services, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home & Accessories.
Data below covers Apple's fiscal years FY2022–FY2024 (Apple fiscal year ends the last Saturday of September).
All figures are officially reported in Apple's Form 10-K filings and quarterly earnings releases.

## Product Or Segment Drivers

1. **iPhone** — largest segment by far; upgrade cycle driven by camera, AI features, and new form factors.
2. **Services** — highest-margin segment; includes App Store, iCloud+, Apple TV+, Apple Music, Apple Pay, AppleCare, search licensing deals (Google), advertising.
3. **Mac** — driven by M-series chip performance; competes in premium laptop and desktop market.
4. **iPad** — relatively flat market; competes with low-end laptops; M4 iPad Pro targets pro use cases.
5. **Wearables, Home & Accessories** — AirPods (dominant wireless headphone market), Apple Watch, HomePod mini, MagSafe accessories.

## Caveats

- Apple does not report gross margin by segment for Mac, iPad, or Wearables (only iPhone and Services gross margin are commonly estimated by analysts).
- Services gross margin has been disclosed as approximately 74% in recent periods.
- Apple's fiscal year ends in late September, not December 31.
