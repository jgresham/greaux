## Platform Drivers

1. **HPC (High Performance Computing)** — AI accelerators (Nvidia, AMD), CPUs (Apple M-series,
   AMD EPYC/Ryzen), hyperscaler custom silicon (Amazon Trainium, Google TPU, Microsoft). Largest
   and fastest-growing platform. Advanced 5nm, 3nm, and 2nm nodes critical for AI chip performance.
2. **Smartphone** — Apple A-series, Qualcomm Snapdragon, MediaTek Dimensity. On-device AI inference
   driving migration to 3nm nodes. Seasonal patterns tied to iPhone release cycles.
3. **IoT** — Edge AI inference chips, wearables (Apple Watch), industrial MCUs, smart home devices.
4. **Automotive** — ADAS processors, EV power management SoCs, in-cabin compute. Long qualification
   cycles; mostly on mature nodes with gradual migration to advanced processes.
5. **DCE & Other** — Digital Consumer Electronics (TV, gaming), and miscellaneous platforms. Secular
   decline in TV/consumer; smallest and slowest-growing category.

## ADS Structure

- TSMC ordinary shares trade on Taiwan Stock Exchange (ticker: 2330)
- TSMC ADSs trade on NYSE (ticker: TSM); 1 ADS = 5 ordinary shares
- CY2024 diluted ordinary shares: ~25,929M → ~5,186M ADS equivalents

## Caveats

- Platform revenue figures are rounded estimates based on percentage breakdowns applied to reported TWD totals.
  TSMC discloses platform percentages; exact USD figures depend on exchange rate assumption.
- TSMC does not report GAAP net income by platform; only total company net income.
- Advanced node percentage thresholds have shifted: TSMC previously defined "advanced" as ≤16nm,
  now uses ≤7nm as the primary disclosure threshold for competitive reasons.
